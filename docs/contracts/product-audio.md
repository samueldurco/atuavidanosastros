# Eligible audio preparation — atv-audio-transcript/1.0.0

Local preparation only, not an audio file, voice approval, publication or provider authorization. The catalog currently marks only `purpose-career` as audio-eligible; that product and all workflow releases remain disabled. Catalog eligibility is not entitlement or release approval.

`prepareProductNarration` accepts only an owner-scoped `read_product_run` projection from a trusted server caller. It reuses the strict reader parser, requires READY/released calculation and reviewed editorial content, and checks the catalog's audio capability. Parsing and SHA-256 are not authentication. Browser-submitted snapshots must never authorize preparation, synthesis or delivery.

The preparation mechanically copies the complete title, ordered editorial headings/text/evidence links, ordered calculation facts with explicit calculated/reported/drawn labels and sources, both ordered limit lists and method/edition versions. It adds fixed product/method/privacy notices but no interpretation, summary, inferred pronunciation, SSML, transliteration or omission. Exact segment boundaries and evidence IDs are retained in a manifest; the plain-text transcript joins their text with two line breaks. Original content is never interpreted as markup or voice commands. A future adapter must treat it as plain text, never concatenate it into SSML or instructions.

UTF-8 transcript size is bounded at 90,000 bytes; unsupported control/bidirectional characters and malformed Unicode refuse the entire preparation, with no truncation or silent cleanup. This is a local resource bound, not a provider cost, character quota or duration estimate. Original newlines, tabs, accents, symbols and supplementary Unicode remain unchanged. Consumers must still test their selected voice's pronunciation and full fidelity.

The result is a `prepared` candidate with publication and synthesis explicitly `blocked`. A transcript digest binds exact text and another digest binds the contract version, run/product/revision, calculation/editorial versions, promotion/review identifiers and complete ordered segment manifest. Those identifiers remain internal and are not spoken. Neither digest is a signature, review receipt, eval pass or permission to transmit personal data. Fresh authorization/gate checks are mandatory before any eventual synthesis, persistence and download; prepared text must not be reused as a durable authorization after revocation or reprocessing.

No provider, model, voice, SDK, request route, queue, scheduler, credential, email, artifact format/policy or UI control is added. No result is persisted or sent externally. Synthesis requires explicit opt-in, an authorized provider/voice and data-processing policy, evaluated pronunciation/fidelity, approved real cost/latency caps and independent final-audio review tied to exact bytes. Storage/recovery/revocation must be added with ownership and publication checks before audio is offered. None of those downstream gates is satisfied by transcript preparation.
