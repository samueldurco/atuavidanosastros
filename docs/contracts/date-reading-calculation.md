# Leitura da Data — persisted sample contract

`date-reading` uses `atv-context-product-calculation/1.0.0`. Its experimental base consists of ten canonical natal positions and ten canonical positions at exactly **12:00 UTC** on the requested Gregorian date, within 1900–2099. The sample is geocentric and does not infer current location or local timezone. Natal civil/UTC correspondence is independently validated before either provider call.

Before editorial preparation, `validDateReadingProjection` inspects the original snapshot, including original fact keys. Evidence preparation `atv-product-editorial-evidence/1.24.0` rejects changed projection contracts, extra fields, missing or reordered positions, invalid numeric ranges, inconsistent engine identity/provenance, mismatched date/sample/JD, changed fact IDs/kinds/displays/sources and missing or altered limitations. It reconstructs the expected twenty position facts and the single sample-instant fact from persisted geometry and provenance. It never calls an ephemeris, recalculates a chart or certifies its origin or accuracy.

An optional final `personal-context` fact remains `reported`, sourced from `input.context`, and preserves the exact submitted text. It must be nonblank, well-formed Unicode, contain no disallowed C0/C1/DEL controls and contain at most 1,200 UTF-16 units. Context does not change natal or sample geometry. The projection does not retain the original birth input or authorize sharing/ATV+ continuity. Source authentication and comparison against the immutable request belong to the trusted processing/persistence boundary; this pure coherence check cannot establish them alone.

The snapshot keeps empty aspects/events, null compatibility score, `not-authorized` sharing and all engine/product warnings. No houses, angles, exact transit search, application/separation, local-day coverage, favorable time, duration or temporal window is available. This partial base does not fulfill the complete Cycles & Time product requirements or homologate the engine. E1 remains partial; E2 requires approved complete content/model and legitimate review, and hosted acceptance requires an available authenticated project. Release gates remain closed.
