# Semana — bounded temporal editorial draft

`atv-week-temporal-editorial/1.0.0` selects an editorial draft profile only after `validWeekTemporalProjection` accepts a complete `atv-week-reading-calculation/1.2.0` snapshot. The profile is partial and independent of the legacy seven-noon-sample profile. It is not model validation, editorial approval or release authorization.

The envelope preserves the eleven calculated facts in order: one seven-day UTC search summary followed by ten per-body nominal event/window counts. A consented declared context may follow as `personal-context`, `kind=reported`, `source=input.context`; it never changes the calculation. Structural validation verifies the summary date, policy label, source version, ten body order and aggregate counts. Geometry and original evidence are verified by the Worker projection before the envelope is formed.

The draft has three hypotheses: frame, five near-body counts, and five far-body counts. Each cites the search summary and its applicable counts; all three cite reported context when present. It has no relations, two ordered syntheses, three practical questions and four literal limits. Those limits distinguish observed candidate counts from continuous occupancy, favorability, intensity, priority, applying/separating, local-day coverage, calendar events and ATV+ continuity. The output budget is three claims, zero relations, 4,000 tokens and 16,000 characters. Structural inspection can request independent editorial review; it cannot declare content useful or approved.

The delivery projection retains the summary, all ten counts, optional reported context, each claim, both syntheses, references and limits. It remains `prepared_for_review` with publication blocked. Legacy 1.0 readings retain their previous grouping and titles. Full E2 requires useful interpretation over the complete product contract and an approved editorial source or validated model/prompt. E5 and hosted release require their separate gates.
